How to play

Friday Night Hangout  ·  September 11, 2026

Jeopardy is an American TV quiz show. The format is a grid: topics across the top, point values down the side, and the bigger the number the harder it is. Someone opens a square, people race to answer, and whoever gets it wins those points for their group.

Jeopardy 是美国的一档电视问答节目。规则很简单：一块格子板，横排是主题， 竖排是分数，分数越高题越难。翻开一格，大家抢答，谁抢到并且答对，分数就归他那一组。

1

Get your group

先拿组号

Type your name, then take your pick. Draw one for me hands you a group. Let me choose lets you take your own: the number you counted off, or whichever group you'd rather be in. Either way you end up in one of four.

输入名字，然后两种方式都行。点 Draw one for me 系统直接给你一个组号；点 Let me choose 你自己挑，报数报到几就选几，或者想跟谁一组就选哪一组。两种方式最后都会落在四个组里的一个。

If you're on Zoom, rename yourself with the number in front, like 2 Kevin, so the host knows which group just raised a hand.

在 Zoom 上的请把自己的名字改成「组号 + 名字」，比如 2 Kevin，这样主持人一看就知道是哪一组举的手。

2

The board

这就是板子

Two boards. The green board on top has five topics: Fitness, Beauty, Home, Amway and Education, each worth 100, 300, 600 and 1000. Green is open to everyone, new or experienced. The orange board below the line has four topics worth 1500 and 2000, and it is 3/6 and above only. We play green first, then orange.

上下两块板。上面绿色那块有五个主题：Fitness、Beauty、Home、Amway、Education， 每个主题 100、300、600、1000 四格，所有人都能抢，新伙伴也一样。橙线以下是橙色那块， 四个主题，1500 和 2000，只有 3/6 以上。先打绿色，再打橙色。

3

Green squares · 20 seconds

绿色格子，20 秒

A question goes up with a 20 second clock. Raise your hand — the button on Zoom, or your actual hand in the room. The fastest hand answers. Right, your group takes the points. Wrong or out of time, your group loses the square and it goes to the next hand up. So raise it when you know it, not when you hope.

题目出来，20 秒倒计时。举手，Zoom 上按举手键，现场就直接举手。最快的那只手来答。 答对，分数归你们组；答错或者超时，这一格你们组就丢了，换下一只手。所以有把握再举。

4

Orange squares · you talk

橙色格子，你来讲

Not a quiz. There's no answer to guess — a topic comes up instead. Same race, hands up, and the first hand stands up and talks about it. 1500 is three minutes, 2000 is five. Deliver it and the points are your group's. Each group takes one 1500 and one 2000, no more.

这两排不是问答，没有标准答案，出来的是一个话题。一样举手抢，抢到的人 站起来讲。1500 讲三分钟，2000 讲五分钟，讲完分数就是你们组的。每组只能拿一个 1500 和一个 2000。
