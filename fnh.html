A quiz game for four groups. The host calls a race, the fastest hand picks a square, and a right answer wins the points for your group.

四个组的抢答游戏。主持人一喊开始，大家抢着举手，最快的人挑一格来答，答对了分数就归你们组。

House rules

规则一览

Two boards

两块板

Green, 100 to 1000, is open to everyone: Fitness, Beauty, Home, Amway and Education. If a question asks for two things, you need both. Orange, 1500 and 2000, is for 3/6 and above: pick a topic and share, 3 minutes for a 1500 and 5 for a 2000. Green runs until 8:35, then orange.

绿色（100 到 1000）所有人都能抢：Fitness、Beauty、Home、Amway、Education。题目要两个答案，就得都答出来。橙色（1500 和 2000）只给 3/6 以上：挑一个话题分享，1500 讲 3 分钟，2000 讲 5 分钟。先打绿色到 8:35，再打橙色。

Room and Zoom take turns

现场和 Zoom 轮流来

The room goes first, then Zoom, then the room again. When one side misses, the question crosses over and the other three groups race for it. If nobody gets it, the host shows the answer and nobody scores.

现场先，然后 Zoom，再回到现场。一边没答对，这题就交给另一边，剩下三组来抢。要是谁都没答出来，主持人公布答案，这一格谁都不得分。

PHP: three per group ❤️❤️❤️

PHP：每组三次 ❤️❤️❤️

On a green square, the player who got picked can call a PHP and huddle with any leader from their group: whisper in the room, private chat on Zoom. The clock keeps running, so the huddle comes out of their own time. The player has to say the answer. If it comes out of a leader's mouth, it doesn't count.

绿色格子上，被点到的人可以叫 PHP，找自己组的任意一位 leader 商量：现场小声说，Zoom 上私聊。计时不停，商量的时间就算在你自己的答题时间里。答案必须由你本人说出来，要是从 leader 嘴里说出来的，不算。

Play it straight

靠诚信来玩

This game runs on integrity and honesty. No Google, no ChatGPT, no AI of any kind. If you know it, you know it.

这个游戏靠的是诚信。不查 Google，不用 ChatGPT，任何 AI 都不用。会就是会。

Part 2 · How the game runs
1Two boards

Two boards tonight. Green, 100 to 1000, is open to everyone, and the bigger the number, the harder the question. Orange, 1500 and 2000, is for 3/6 and above. We get there at 8:35.

Tech: Next ▶

2Hands up on 1

I count 3, 2, 1. On 1, hands up. On Zoom, use Raise Hand. If your hand goes up before 1, it doesn't count. The room and Zoom take turns.

Tech: Next ▶

3First hand picks any square

The first hand picks any square. Any topic, any number, you don't have to start at 100.

Tech: Next ▶

4The host reads, then the clock starts

I read the question out, and then the clock starts: 20 seconds on the 100s and 300s, 35 on the 600s and 1000s. If it asks for two things, I need both.

Tech: Next ▶

5Right: your group scores

Get it right, and the points go to your group. Keep an eye on the top: whoever's in front wears the crown.

Tech: Next ▶

6Wrong or out of time: it crosses over

Get it wrong or run out of time, and your group loses the square. It crosses to the other side, and the other three groups race for it. If nobody gets it, I show the answer and the square closes with no points. So put your hand up when you know it, not when you hope.

Tech: Next ▶

7Stuck? Call a PHP

Stuck? You have a lifeline: PHP. Every group gets three, the red hearts under your score. Say "PHP" and huddle with one of your leaders: whisper in the room, private chat on Zoom. The clock does not stop, and the answer has to come out of your mouth. If your leader says it, it doesn't count. Leaders, keep your Zoom chat open.

Tech: Next ▶

8Orange squares: you talk

The orange squares aren't a quiz. 3/6 and above, you pick a topic and share: three minutes for a 1500, five for a 2000. Deliver it, and the points are your group's. One of each per group.

Tech: Next ▶

9Play it straight

One more thing, and it matters. Some of our leaders have seen these questions, so tonight runs on integrity and honesty. No Google, no ChatGPT, no AI of any kind. If you know it, you know it.
