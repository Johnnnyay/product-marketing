The four groups

How it works. Four categories, five squares each, and you raise your hand on every row. On 100, 300 and 500 anyone can go for it. First hand answers, 20 seconds on the clock, and get it wrong and your group loses the square — it goes to the floor and the other groups fight over it. So put your hand up when you know it, not when you hope. On 800 and 1000 only 3/6 and above may raise a hand, and there is nothing to guess: the first hand up stands and delivers on the topic. One 800 and one 1000 per group.

How to play

怎么玩  ·  Friday Night Hangout  ·  September 11

Jeopardy is an American TV quiz show. The format is a grid: topics across the top, point values down the side, and the bigger the number the harder it is. Someone opens a square, people race to answer, and whoever gets it wins those points for their group.

Jeopardy 是美国的一档电视问答节目。规则很简单：一块格子板，横排是主题， 竖排是分数，分数越高题越难。翻开一格，大家抢答，谁抢到并且答对，分数就归他那一组。

1

Get your group

先拿组号

Type your name and you get a group number, 1 to 4. Everyone is in one of four groups. If you're on Zoom, rename yourself with the number in front, like 2 Kevin, so the host knows which group just raised a hand.

输入名字就会拿到一个组号，1 到 4，每个人都在四个组里的一个。在 Zoom 上的请把自己的名字 改成「组号 + 名字」，比如 2 Kevin，这样主持人一看就知道是哪一组举的手。

2

The board

这就是板子

Four topics across, five rows down. The green rows are open to everyone, new or experienced. The orange rows below the line are 3/6 and above only. Twenty squares, and we play until they're gone.

横排四个主题，竖排五个分数。绿色那三排所有人都能抢，新伙伴也一样。 橙线以下的橙色两排只有 3/6 以上。一共二十格，打完为止。

3

Green squares · 20 seconds

绿色格子，20 秒

A question goes up with a 20 second clock. Raise your hand — the button on Zoom, or your actual hand in the room. The fastest hand answers. Right, your group takes the points. Wrong or out of time, your group loses the square and it goes to the next hand up. So raise it when you know it, not when you hope.

题目出来，20 秒倒计时。举手，Zoom 上按举手键，现场就直接举手。最快的那只手来答。 答对，分数归你们组；答错或者超时，这一格你们组就丢了，换下一只手。所以有把握再举。

4

Orange squares · you talk

橙色格子，你来讲

Not a quiz. There's no answer to guess — a topic comes up instead. Same race, hands up, and the first hand stands up and talks about it. 800 is three minutes, 1000 is five. Deliver it and the points are your group's. Each group takes one 800 and one 1000, no more.

这两排不是问答，没有标准答案，出来的是一个话题。一样举手抢，抢到的人 站起来讲。800 讲三分钟，1000 讲五分钟，讲完分数就是你们组的。每组只能拿一个 800 和一个 1000。
