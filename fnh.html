How to play

Friday Night Hangout  ·  September 11, 2026

A quiz game for four groups. The host calls a race, the fastest hand picks a square, and a right answer wins the points for your group.

四个组的抢答游戏。主持人一喊开始大家抢着举手，最快的人挑一格来答，答对了分数就归你们组。

The whole game in four moves

整个游戏就四步

1

Get a group

先拿组号

On the participant page, draw a group or choose one. On Zoom, put the number in front of your name, like 2 Kevin.

在 participant page 输入名字，抽一个组或者自己选。Zoom 上把组号加在名字前面，比如 2 Kevin。

2

Hands up on 1

喊到 1 再举手

The host counts 3, 2, 1. On 1, hands up. On Zoom, use Raise Hand. A hand before 1 doesn't count.

主持人喊 3、2、1，喊到 1 才举手，Zoom 上点 Raise Hand。抢跑不算。

3

Pick any square

任选一格

The first hand picks any topic, any number. The host reads the question out, and then your 20 seconds start.

最快举手的人挑一格，哪个主题、多少分都行。主持人把题读完，20 秒才开始算。

4

Score, or it passes

答对得分，答错换人

Right: the points are your group's. Wrong or out of time: your group loses the square, and the other side gets a shot.

答对，分数归你们组。答错或超时，这一格你们组就丢了，交给另一边来抢。

Room and Zoom take turns. The room goes first, then Zoom, then the room. When one side misses, the question crosses over and the other three groups race for it.
现场和 Zoom 轮流来，现场先。一边没答对，这题就交给另一边，剩下三组来抢。

Play it straight. This game runs on integrity and honesty. No Google, no ChatGPT, no AI of any kind. If you know it, you know it.
这个游戏靠的是诚信。不查 Google，不用 ChatGPT，任何 AI 都不用。会就是会。

Quick answers

常见问题

Do we have to start at 100?

No. Pick any square, in any order. A bigger number is a harder question and more points.

不用。哪格都能挑，不分顺序。分数越高题越难，拿到的分也越多。

Is every square a different question?

Yes. Every square has its own question, and nobody sees it until the host reads it out.

是的，每一格都是不同的题，主持人读出来之前谁都看不到。

When does the 20 seconds start?

After the host finishes reading. Listen first, then answer.

主持人把题读完才开始计时。先听完，再回答。

What if we get it wrong?

Your group loses that square. It crosses to the other side (room to Zoom, or Zoom to room), the host counts 3, 2, 1 again, and the other three groups race for it.

这一格你们组就丢了。题交给另一边（现场到 Zoom，或者 Zoom 到现场），主持人再喊一次 3、2、1，剩下三组来抢。

What if nobody gets it?

The host reads the answer out and nobody scores on that one.

主持人公布答案，这一格谁都不得分。

I'm on Zoom. How do I raise my hand?

Reactions, then Raise Hand. Zoom lists hands in the order they went up, so the first hand really is first. Lower it after your turn.

点 Reactions 里的 Raise Hand。Zoom 会按举手先后排序，谁先举谁就排第一。轮完记得把手放下。

What if my hand goes up before "1"?

It doesn't count. Wait for 1.

不算，等主持人喊到 1。

Can I look it up?

No. No Google, no ChatGPT, no AI of any kind. Some leaders have seen the questions, so tonight runs on integrity and honesty. If you know it, you know it.

不能。不查 Google，不用 ChatGPT，任何 AI 都不用。有些 leader 提前看过题，所以大家靠诚信。会就是会。

I'm new. Can I play?

Yes. The whole green board is open to everyone, first night or fifth year. The orange board is for 3/6 and above.

当然可以。绿色整块板所有人都能抢，第一次来也一样。橙色那块是 3/6 以上的。

Do I need to sign up for the orange squares?

No. Same race: 3/6 and above raise a hand, and the first hand picks a topic.

不用报名，一样抢：3/6 以上的举手，最快的人挑话题。

Where do I see the board?

On the big screen in the room, and shared on Zoom. The scores sit across the top, and the group in front wears the crown. Your phone is only for getting your group.

现场看大屏幕，Zoom 上看共享屏幕。分数在最上面，领先的那组头上有皇冠。手机只用来拿组号。

How it works · 90 sec

Here's the whole game in four moves.

One. I count 3, 2, 1. On 1, hands up. On Zoom, use Raise Hand. If your hand goes up before 1, it doesn't count.

Two. The first hand picks any square. Any topic, any number, you don't have to start at 100. Bigger number, harder question, more points.

Three. I read the question out, and then you have 20 seconds. If it asks for two things, I need both.

Four. Get it right, and the points go to your group. Get it wrong or run out of time, and your group loses the square. It crosses to the other side. The room and Zoom take turns, so if the room misses, the other three groups on Zoom race for it, and the other way round.

So put your hand up when you know it, not when you hope.
