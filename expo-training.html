Pain-Point Specialist Course痛点专家课

Right now only Johnny and Irene can read a check-in and recommend with confidence. This course turns each common pain point into a simple draft any of us can use in two weeks: a three-step conversation and a map of which product goes with what the guest marked. Oct 30 is where we practise it for real.

现在能看懂问卷、放心推荐产品的，只有Johnny和Irene两个人。这门课把最常见的几个痛点，各自做成一份两周就能上手的草稿：三步聊法，加一张对照表，客人勾了什么就推荐什么。10月30日，就是我们真正上场练习的地方。

The goal目标

Oct 30 is our practice field, not a sales target

We don't have volume pressure right now, and that's the opportunity: room to practise and become experts. Nobody needs to hit a number that night. The win is that each of us owns one part of the night and guides a guest through it on our own, without Johnny or Irene stepping in.

Start with the most common pain points

From the check-ins guests filled in on Sep 25 and before: tired, poor sleep, acne and weight. More modules follow the same shape (gut, liver, dull skin, immunity).

10月30日是练兵场，不是业绩指标

最近没有业绩压力，这反而是机会：有空间去练，去成为专家。那天晚上谁都不用冲数字。我们要的胜利，是每个人负责当晚的一个环节，自己带客人走完，不需要Johnny或Irene出手。

先从最常见的痛点开始

看9月25日和之前客人填的问卷，最多的是累、睡不好、痘痘和体重。之后的模块都按同样的结构来做（肠道、肝、暗沉、免疫力）。

The 3 steps三步聊法

Every module is the same three steps. Each one has a draft to say, in English and Chinese. Make it yours.

每个模块都是同样的三步，每一步都有现成的草稿，中英文都有。用你自己的话说出来就好。

  1. 1
    I know how you feelSay back what they marked on the check-in, in their words.
  2. 2
    I felt the same, or someone I know didA line or two of your own story, or someone close to you. No story yet? "A lot of the people I talk to say exactly the same thing." Never make one up.
  3. 3
    Here's what I found, and you should knowTwo or three plain sentences on what's going on, then the product map: what they marked, the product that goes with it, and the ask.
  1. 1
    我懂你的感受用对方自己的话，把问卷上勾的说回去。
  2. 2
    我也有过，或者我身边的人有过一两句你自己的故事，或者身边人的。还没有故事？就说"我身边很多朋友也是这样"。千万别编。
  3. 3
    我后来学到的，你也该知道两三句大白话讲清楚是怎么回事，然后看产品对照表：对方勾了什么，对应什么产品，然后开口。

The walk-through on Oct 3010月30日当天流程

One guest, one specialist, from hello to checkout. About 15 to 20 minutes. Health sits next to XS, skin on the other side. Booked and on time goes next; everyone else joins the waitlist in order.

  1. Greet and set the frame (1 min): "Who invited you? Tonight we'll look at your check-in together, and if something clicks you can grab it at checkout."
  2. Read the check-in (3 min): pick the one or two things they marked often, and say them back (step 1).
  3. Explain and show (5 min): steps 2 and 3 from your module, the quick guide on your phone, the demo.
  4. Recommend and ask (3 min): the product map row that matches, the price list, then the ask. Wait.
  5. Close or release (2 min): yes, walk them to Wendy with the product in hand. No, note who invited them, and go get your next guest.

A guest with a pain point that isn't yours? Bring in the teammate who owns it, and watch how they do it.

一位客人，一位专家，从打招呼到结账，大约15到20分钟。健康区挨着XS，皮肤区在另一边。预约了且准时到的客人先来，其他人按顺序排队。

  1. 打招呼，说清楚今晚怎么进行（1分钟）："是谁邀请你来的？今晚我们一起看看你的问卷，如果有觉得对的，结账时可以直接带走。"
  2. 看问卷（3分钟）：挑出对方勾了"经常"的一两项，用对方的话说回去（第1步）。
  3. 讲清楚，做演示（5分钟）：用你模块里的第2、3步，手机上打开快速了解，再做演示。
  4. 推荐，开口（3分钟）：对照表里对得上的那一项，给价格单，然后开口。说完就等对方回答。
  5. 成交或放手（2分钟）：答应了，就拿着产品陪对方去Wendy那里结账。没答应，记下邀请人，去接下一位客人。

客人的痛点不是你负责的？请负责这个痛点的队友过来，你在旁边看对方怎么带。

Asking for the sale开口要单

There's no number to hit on Oct 30, so this is the place to practise the ask. Irene's rule: there's no secret strategy. You ask, and if it's a no, the world still turns, and you just got a rep in. What we were missing on Sep 25 was the ask itself.

When they sayYou say
"How much is it?"Show the price list. "This is the price. Do you want to take it home tonight?"
"I'll think about it.""Totally. What's the part you're not sure about?" If still no: "No problem, who invited you? They'll check in with you next week."
"I'm just here for fun.""Love that. Want the two-minute version of your check-in?"
"I already take vitamins.""Great, what are you taking? Let's see if it covers what you marked."
"Too many things."Drop to one product. "Then start with just this one."
Not in stock tonightTake the order at checkout anyway. We place it after.

Don't: talk 20 minutes to build the relationship, hand over a menu of five bottles, leave the follow-up to the guest, or promise results. These are foods, not drugs.

10月30日没有数字要冲，所以正好用来练开口。Irene说得好：没有什么秘诀，就是开口问；被拒绝了，地球照样转，你还多练了一次。9月25日那场，我们缺的就是开口这一步。

客人说你这样回
"多少钱？"把价格单给对方看。"价格是这样的。今天要不要带回去？"
"我再想想。""当然。主要是哪一点还不确定？"如果还是不要："没关系，是谁邀请你来的？下周让对方再跟你聊。"
"我就是来玩的。""太好了。要不要花两分钟，听听你的问卷结果？"
"我已经在吃维生素了。""很好，吃的是什么？我们看看能不能照顾到你勾的那几项。"

当晚没货照样在结账处下单，活动结束后统一订货。

别这样做：为了建立关系聊上20分钟；一下推五瓶；让客人自己去跟进；承诺效果。这些是食品，不是药。

Demos演示

Vitamin C apple

  • Slice an apple into small pieces (bring a fruit knife)
  • Coat half with dissolved vitamin C
  • After 10 to 15 minutes the plain slices brown, the vitamin C ones stay fresh
Bridge

"Vitamin C is the body's main antioxidant. This is what it does on an apple."

XS mocktail

  • XS, lemon, lime and mint in a cup
  • Two cans with a ribbon at 2 for $5
