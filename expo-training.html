The goal

Module 1Tired all the time, and sleeping badly

Who it's for: guests who mark tired, afternoon crash, sleepy after meals, hard to fall asleep, waking at night, waking up unrefreshed, or stressed. The two travel together: poor sleep makes the day tired, and a tired, wired day makes the night worse.

Study: Energy quick guideSleep quick guide · then tap "Full 4-step plan" on each · KB lessons 03 (sleep debt), 13 (sleep cycles), 16 and 17 (hidden hunger), 22 and 23 (B vitamins), 20 and 21 (protein), 37 (minerals).

1Know it

  • Sleepy after lunch is a sugar spike and crash: carbs alone turn into sugar fast, insulin rushes out, blood sugar drops, you crash.
  • Drained after work is a body that burned through its raw materials: B vitamins, iron, magnesium and protein turn food into energy, and running short sends no hunger signal.
  • Coffee borrows energy. It hides the tired signal and you pay it back later. Two weeks of six-hour nights leaves you as foggy as two nights awake, and you don't notice.
  • Tired but wired is a stress system that never stands down at night. Stress also uses up magnesium.
  • Late nights, takeout and drinks keep the liver working overtime, which leaves you heavy and tired even after sleep.

2Manage it

  • Clear: stop eating carbs alone; last coffee before 2 pm; phone out of the bedroom.
  • Regulate: same wake time 7 days a week, 7+ hours; coffee and tea an hour away from meals (they block iron).
  • Replenish: protein, a third of it at breakfast (60 kg means about 72 g a day); B vitamins; magnesium; iron only after a ferritin test.
  • Sustain: the same breakfast 7 to 9 am; the evening routine of Liver Support and Vitamin B around 7:30 to 8:40 pm, judged over months.
  • Doctor first if tired for more than a few weeks, or with weight loss, breathlessness, very heavy periods, low mood, loud snoring or pauses in breathing.

Quiz

Someone gets sleepy after lunch. What's going on, in one sentence?

A sugar spike and crash: carbs alone turn into sugar fast, insulin rises, blood sugar drops.

Two ways to slow the sugar down without giving up rice?

Eat vegetables and protein first and the rice last; add fibre; Carb Blocker before the meal; swap one meal for a protein shake.

Why doesn't coffee fix tiredness?

It borrows energy and hides the tired signal; the sleep debt is still there.

What do two weeks of six-hour nights do?

Leave you about as impaired as two nights with no sleep, without you noticing.

Which raw materials turn food into energy?

B vitamins, iron, magnesium and protein.

Who should get ferritin checked before taking iron?

Women who are tired all the time, especially with heavy periods; vegetarians; frequent blood donors.

Protein target for someone who weighs 60 kg?

About 72 g a day (1.2 g per kg), a third of it at breakfast.

What is in Sleep Gummies, and who shouldn't take them?

3 mg melatonin with passionflower. Occasional nights only; not for children, pregnancy, breastfeeding or people on blood thinners; no driving after.

What is the evening liver routine?

Liver Support and Vitamin B together around 7:30 to 8:40 pm, every night, judged over months (liver cells renew in about 180 days).

When do you send them to a doctor first?

Tired for weeks, or with weight loss, breathlessness, very heavy periods, low mood, loud snoring or breathing pauses.

Module 2Acne and breakouts

Who it's for: guests who mark breakouts, oily skin, breakouts before their period, marks left after pimples, or breakouts with stress.

Study: Acne & Skin quick guideCycle quick guide · then the full 4-step plan · Irene's skin guide sheet · KB sections 4 (liver) and 5 (gut), lessons 41 (fibre), 42 (probiotics and skin), 29 (vitamin C), 24 (B2), 31 (vitamin E).

1Know it

  • A pore becomes a pimple when four things line up: too much oil, a plug, bacteria in the trapped oil, and inflammation. Skincare handles the last two.
  • The first two are set inside: sugar spikes raise insulin and IGF-1, which turn up oil; a gut out of balance adds inflammation; stress and short sleep turn up oil too.
  • Before the period, hormones shift and breakouts show up on the jaw and chin, on schedule.
  • Marks are mostly pigment left after inflammation, not scars. Picking and sun make them darker.
  • Washing harder backfires. Stripped skin makes more oil.

2Manage it

  • Clear: stop the sugar spike (don't quit rice, slow it down); fibre and probiotics for the gut; liver support for the load.
  • Regulate: a gentle cleanser; salicylic acid toner on the whole area; patch, don't pick.
  • Replenish: protein (skin rebuilds with it), vitamin C, B vitamins.
  • Sustain: SPF every day; sleep; skin renews about monthly, so judge it over two or three months.
  • Doctor first for deep painful lumps, scarring, acne with irregular periods or extra hair growth, or breakouts affecting their mood.

Quiz

What four things turn a pore into a pimple?

Too much oil, a plug, bacteria in the trapped oil, inflammation.

Which of them are set inside the body?

Oil output (sugar spikes, insulin and IGF-1, stress and sleep) and inflammation (gut, liver load).

Why salicylic acid in the toner?

It dissolves in oil, so it gets inside the pore and breaks the plug up.

Why patch instead of pick?

Picking is the main cause of marks, and marks last months longer than the pimple.

Breakouts before the period: what helps and how is it taken?

Cycle Support, every day through the month, judged over about three cycles. Ask first if pregnant, trying, or on hormonal birth control.

What stops marks from getting darker?

Not picking, sunscreen every day, and vitamin C.

Oily skin: wash more?

No. Stripped skin makes more oil. Wash gentler and hydrate.

Why the liver in an acne plan?

In the teaching we follow, the liver and gut clear what the skin would otherwise show; the liver also clears used hormones.

The one line for breakouts after takeout?

"You don't have to quit rice, you just stop the spike."

When do you send them to a doctor or dermatologist first?

Deep painful lumps, scarring, acne with irregular periods or extra hair growth, or breakouts affecting their mood.

Module 3Weight management and protein

Who it's for: guests who mark weight gain, a diet that worked then came back, snacking at night, stress eating, belly fat, or wanting to lose fat without losing muscle.

Study: Weight quick guideMuscle quick guide · then the full 4-step plan · KB lessons 20 and 21 (protein), 14 (exercise is not the same as health), 41 (fibre).

1Know it

  • The scale can't tell fat from muscle. Cut hard and up to a third of what you lose can be muscle, and muscle burns energy at rest. So the weight that comes back is fat.
  • Carbs alone spike insulin, and insulin is also the signal to store. Small portions of rice, noodles or juice still add up.
  • Night snacking starts in the morning: skip breakfast, rush lunch, and the body chases it at night. Short sleep turns hunger up.
  • Stress pushes cravings toward sugar and fat. It's not a willpower problem.
  • You can't choose where fat comes off. Belly fat moves with the whole routine, not sit-ups.

2Manage it

  • Clear: slow the sugar: vegetables and protein first, rice last; fibre.
  • Regulate: fix breakfast, sleep and stress, not just the evening.
  • Replenish: enough protein so the body has no reason to touch muscle (about 1.2 g per kg a day, more for people who train); a multivitamin when eating less.
  • Sustain: two strength sessions a week; measure the waist, not just the scale.
  • Doctor first if weight changes fast for no clear reason, or with constant tiredness, feeling cold, strong thirst or missed periods.

3Consult it

Ask: "What have you tried before, and what happened after?" · "What's breakfast usually?" · "Any strength training?"

Quiz

Why does weight come back after a crash diet?

Up to a third of the loss can be muscle, muscle burns energy at rest, so you finish lighter but burning less, and what returns is fat.

How do you slow the sugar without giving up rice?

Vegetables and protein first, rice last; fibre; Carb Blocker before a rice meal.

Protein target for someone who weighs 70 kg?

About 84 g a day (1.2 g per kg), more if they strength-train.

Where does night snacking usually start?

At breakfast: skipping it or eating carbs only leaves the body chasing it at night.

What tells you more than the scale?

The waist measurement and how clothes fit.

When do you send them to a doctor first?

Fast weight change for no clear reason, or with constant tiredness, feeling cold, strong thirst or missed periods.

The walk-through on Oct 30

One guest, one specialist, from hello to checkout. About 15 to 20 minutes. Health sits next to XS, skin on the other side. Booked and on time goes next; everyone else joins the waitlist in order.

  1. Greet and set the frame (1 min): "Who invited you? Tonight we'll look at your check-in together, and if something clicks you can grab it at checkout."
  2. Read the check-in (3 min): pick the one or two things they marked often. Ask "When does it hit you most?"
  3. Explain and show (5 min): the one line from your module, the quick guide on your phone, the demo.
  4. Recommend and ask (3 min): one bundle plus one add-on, the price list, then the ask. Wait.
  5. Close or release (2 min): yes, walk them to Wendy with the product in hand. No, note who invited them, and go get your next guest.

A guest with a pain point that isn't yours? Bring in the teammate who owns it, and watch how they do it.

Asking for the sale

There's no number to hit on Oct 30, so this is the place to practise the ask. Irene's rule: there's no secret strategy. You ask, and if it's a no, the world still turns, and you just got a rep in. What we were missing on Sep 25 was the ask itself.
