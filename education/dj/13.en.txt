# Gut Health and Heart and Brain Disease

Hello, friends. My name is Zhang Li, 74 years old this year, an attending physician in internal medicine and a senior national nutritionist. In more than thirty years of medical practice, I have persisted in regulating various chronic diseases from the perspective of nutrition, and I have accumulated some experience. Today I am very glad to share with you the topic of gut health and cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease.

Why talk about this topic? Because cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease is now the disease with the highest incidence in our country. In recent years, cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease in our country has not only been rising but also getting younger. China's health big data shows that the number of people with cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease has reached 290 million, including 270 million with [[high blood pressure|高血压, hypertension. The WHO describes it as a major cause of premature death worldwide; it can burst or block the arteries that supply the brain, causing a stroke.|https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hypertension]], 13 million with stroke, 11 million with coronary heart disease, 100 million with high blood lipids, and nearly 100 million with diabetes. Seventy percent of people are at risk of death from overwork, and the number of people who die of cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease in our country each year is as high as 3.5 million. How do you feel when you see these numbers? Are you not very frightened? Among the celebrities we know, Gu Yue died at 68, Gao Xiumin died of myocardial infarction at 46, and Hou Yaowen died suddenly at 59. There are also many workplace elites who died young of cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease, and such reports are common. Here is an example from my own circle: a classmate of my nephew was only in his twenties and very fat. To lose weight he exercised excessively on a treadmill and suddenly collapsed and died. There is no need to give more examples. I think every friend has met quite a few such examples. Cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease has already been wandering around us like a ghost.

What are the risk factors for cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease? They mainly include two aspects, controllable and uncontrollable factors. The uncontrollable factors are heredity, age, and gender. The controllable factors are mainly bad lifestyle, including smoking, heavy drinking, long-term staying up late, and a diet high in three things and low in one: high fat, high salt, high sugar, and low fiber. What is the root cause of cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease? There is a book called The Cellular Factor, and the author points out sharply that the health crisis comes from two factors: widespread chronic poisoning and malnutrition at the cellular level. Here we mainly talk about how a bad lifestyle causes the body's chronic poisoning. A bad lifestyle increases the garbage in the gut and reduces the beneficial bacteria, breaking the flora balance between beneficial and harmful bacteria in the gut, and lowering the gut's cleaning function. The gut's lining becomes inflamed, and the gut wall leaks. The gut's toxins then pass through the leaking gut wall with the blood, and through the liver's portal vein enter the blood vessels of the whole body, causing inflammation of the inner lining of the blood vessels. The garbage in the vessels attaches to the vessel walls and forms [[plaque|斑块, the buildup of fat, cholesterol and other substances on artery walls, a key feature of atherosclerosis, which hardens and narrows the arteries.|https://www.health.harvard.edu/cholesterol-and-lipids/atherosclerosis-symptoms-and-treatments]]. If one day a large plaque blocks a vessel or breaks off and moves with the blood, it blocks the vessel. A blockage in the brain is what we call cerebral infarction. A blockage in the coronary artery of the heart is called myocardial infarction. If a cerebral vessel ruptures because of hardening, that is cerebral hemorrhage. It is like the garbage in our home's drain getting more and more, until one day the pipe is blocked or bursts from long-term corrosion.

The Greek physician and father of modern medicine, Hippocrates, proposed more than two thousand years ago that all disease originates in the gut, and that up to 90 percent of all known human diseases can be traced back to an unhealthy gut. So how do we prevent cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease? First, of course, is the mindset. Cars, positions, and so on are all things outside the body. If you lose your health, what use are these things? Also, we must follow the four cornerstones of health: an optimistic mindset, second, appropriate exercise, third, enough sleep, and fourth, more importantly, balanced nutrition, which can fundamentally prevent the occurrence of cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease.
