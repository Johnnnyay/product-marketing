# A Gut Health Plan You Can Follow

Hello, friends. I am Ling Min, from Liaoyang. In the last lesson we talked about pairing nutrients for gut health. Many friends asked whether there is a concrete plan, and the answer is yes. Today we recommend several effective solutions for gut health management. The plans have all been proven through a large number of people in practice to be simple, safe, and effective. Among them there is daily maintenance and regular regulation, and you can choose according to your own actual situation.

Insufficient intake causes constipation and disordered gut function. Adults should consume 25 to 30 grams of [[dietary fiber|膳食纤维, the indigestible parts of plant foods that add bulk to the stool and feed beneficial gut bacteria.|https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002470.htm]] a day, which can lower the risk of death by 15 percent, but surveys show that the average dietary fiber intake of Chinese people is only 10.8 grams, far below half of the recommended amount. The purpose of clearing the gut is to increase the intake of dietary fiber to promote bowel movement, increase stool volume, wrap up the toxins in the gut, and carry them out of the body, achieving the effect of cleaning the gut. We recommend doing this plan once a month, and the number of days each time is determined by age. For example, at 20 years old you do two days, at 30 three days, at 40 four days, at 50 five days, and so on. The method is: 20 chewable fruit and vegetable fiber tablets a day, divided into two times, 10 tablets each time, each soaked in 1,000 milliliters of water. Drink slowly after they dissolve, finishing between 7 a.m. and 12 p.m. in the morning, and between 12 p.m. and 6 p.m. in the afternoon. After dissolving, be sure to drink in small, slow sips, and you will feel full in the stomach.

In addition, fifteen minutes before, take three kinds of probiotics, three packets each: compound probiotics, women's probiotics, and children's probiotics, to raise the occupancy of beneficial bacteria in the gut, reduce the proportion of harmful bacteria, promote our gut health, and prevent leaky gut.

Plan two is the twenty-day flora replacement plan. Through just twenty days of regulation, you can initially improve the gut flora, prevent leaky gut, and thus improve the health of the brain and body. The twenty-day flora replacement plan has two stages. The first stage is a seven-day regulation period, using three kinds of probiotics: ten packets each of compound probiotics, women's probiotics, and children's probiotics, thirty packets in total, plus ten packets of collagen peptides and one packet of a hot drink, taken together for a week. A warm reminder: in the first two days, you may have strong diarrhea, but with no discomfort, which is just the flora being replaced. The second stage is a fourteen-day consolidation period, using two packets each of the three kinds of probiotics, six packets in total, six packets of collagen peptides, and twenty lecithin capsules, taken for another fourteen days. For later maintenance, you can use the three probiotics plus three peptides plus twenty lecithin capsules for at least three weeks.

For the method, we recommend using a shaker cup. In the first stage, first use about 200 milliliters of warm water at about 40 degrees to mix one packet of the hot drink with ten packets of collagen peptides, then pour in the other thirty packets of probiotics and mix evenly. The thickness can be adjusted to your liking, that is, by how much water you add. The best time to drink is half an hour before bed, all at once, or you can drink it in portions according to your body's condition. In the second stage, because the products are reduced, the amount of water can also be reduced accordingly. The lecithin can be crushed and taken together with the probiotics and collagen peptides.

Through the gut health management plans above, the risk of leaky gut will be lowered, and you will also gain healthy stools, that is, stools that meet the following six conditions: banana-colored, banana-shaped, a slight smell, taking about five minutes, weighing about 300 grams, and once a day. In addition, you will have precise sleep, feeling sleepy on time and sleeping deeply, and feeling full of energy when you wake up, skin that is full and glowing, facial spots that become lighter, skin that becomes whiter, and stronger resistance. For more improvement in the body, you need to verify and feel it yourself. Before you start the gut health management plan, be sure to consult your health consultant first.

For the gut-clearing plan, we recommend doing it once a month, and for the twenty-day flora replacement plan, we recommend doing it every half year. Besides these two regular gut management plans, what everyone should value even more is daily maintenance of gut health. You can add a packet of fiber powder with meals to make up for insufficient dietary fiber intake, or add a high-protein, high-fiber hot drink bottle containing fifteen kinds of quality nutrients between meals. We also strongly recommend that everyone use the health meal every day for daily gut health maintenance.
