As we all know, our diseases and sub-health are directly related to the obesity of our body. First, let us share the harm of obesity. First, obesity can lead to metabolic diseases like gout and type 2 diabetes. Second, obesity is closely related to high blood pressure: a moderately obese person has a five-times-higher probability of developing high blood pressure than a person of normal weight. Third, obesity increases the risk of cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease. Fourth, obesity can cause cancer, mainly tumors of the digestive system and tumors related to female hormones. Fifth, obesity can cause joint disease. Sixth, obesity can cause endocrine disorders, causing ovulation or ejaculation problems and lowering fertility. Eighth, obesity affects the health of the next generation: an obese mother tends to give birth prematurely and increases the child's risk of obesity in childhood. Ninth, obesity also causes psychological disorders. Tenth, obesity can cause liver and kidney disease, such as fatty liver and a large amount of protein in the urine. So obesity is not just an unattractive figure. More importantly, obesity is already a disease.

Obesity is divided into visible obesity and invisible obesity. Visible obesity is too much subcutaneous fat and a high body-fat rate. Invisible obesity is too much [[visceral fat|内脏脂肪, the fat stored deep inside the abdomen around the organs. Unlike the fat just under the skin, it is linked to a higher risk of metabolic disease.|https://www.health.harvard.edu/staying-healthy/taking-aim-at-belly-fat]], and too much visceral fat seriously affects your health. Obesity will surpass smoking as the next killer threatening health, so losing weight cannot be delayed.

What other weight-loss methods are harmful to the body? Let us talk about the dieting we often mention. Relying only on dieting for weight loss works in the short term, but in the long term it is very difficult. What is the reason? You already do not eat much. If you force yourself to diet, over time it causes malnutrition. Once resistance drops, disease occurs, and the person naturally chooses to compensate with more food to maintain the body's resistance. So you fall into a yo-yo effect of losing two jin and gaining four, losing ten jin and gaining twenty. In this sense, relying only on dieting cannot achieve your weight-loss goal.

There is also the common drug-based weight loss. Common weight-loss drugs can be divided into three types. The first type acts on the brain, suppressing appetite so you do not feel hungry. The second type speeds up excretion, reducing the time food stays in the gut. The third type inhibits absorption, inhibiting the small intestine's absorption of nutrients like fat. No matter which of the three types, they are all drugs, and all drugs carry some toxicity, causing damage to our body. So here I advise everyone that when choosing a weight-loss method, you must choose a healthy and safe method, not a method that sacrifices health.
