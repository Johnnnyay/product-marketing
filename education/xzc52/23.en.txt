# The B Vitamins and Vitamin B1

Third, anything to do with the nervous system is related to the B vitamins, so the B group is also the first choice for nourishing the nerves.

Those are the three major functions of the B vitamins, and every other function extends from them: preventing anemia, for example, preventing hardening of the arteries, and preventing neural tube defects. Preventing anemia is related to [[B12 and folic acid|Vitamin B12 and folate are both needed to form red blood cells and DNA. Now let us look at B1 within the B group. The encyclopedia and the university textbooks give a lot of data on nutrients. Vitamin B1, for example, promotes growth, assists carbohydrate metabolism, aids digestion and improves mental state. So big emotions, heavy stress and a hot temper are certainly related to B1. If you feel your mood is off, that you are negative, low and uncomfortable, even withdrawn, anything to do with mood and mental state is really related to a shortage of vitamin B1. B1 also prevents [[beriberi|脚气病, beriberi, the disease of thiamin (B1) deficiency. Dry beriberi is a nerve disease: symmetrical numbness, pain and weakness in the limbs. Wet beriberi is a heart disease with high-output heart failure, swelling and rapid pulse, and the fulminant form can be fatal within hours or days without thiamin. In Chinese the word for beriberi differs by a single character from 脚气, athlete's foot, hence the speaker's warning not to confuse them. The US RDA for thiamin is 1.2 mg for men and 1.1 mg for women.|https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3841631/]]. And here some people confuse athlete's foot with beriberi. That is wrong.

Beriberi, in medicine, is a systemic disease caused by vitamin B1 deficiency. Dry beriberi includes ascending and even symmetrical peripheral neuritis, sensory and motor disturbance, and loss of muscle strength. Wet beriberi shows as weakness, fatigue, palpitations and shortness of breath, and even, because of right ventricular failure, loss of appetite, nausea, vomiting, reduced urine and peripheral edema.

Then there is acute fulminant cardiovascular beriberi, with acute circulatory failure, heavy and short breath, agitation and a drop in blood pressure. The patient can die of acute heart failure within hours or days. That is beriberi. Athlete's foot is a common skin disease caused by a fungus. They are two different things.
