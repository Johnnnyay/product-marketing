The way things work today is that people get sick, then go looking for a doctor, then go to the hospital and depend on the doctor. But what is the real logic underneath disease? Do you know?

The truth is that the deepest principles are simple. Over more than twenty years of health management, across a huge number of cases, and drawing on the collective wisdom of many experts in the field, we discovered something. Whether it is the three highs of blood pressure, blood sugar and blood lipids, whether it is diabetes or cancer, an autoimmune disease, nodules or polyps, behind all of them there is one and the same cause. What is it?

All of these problems come down to two causes. The first is toxin overload. Toxins accumulate in the body year after year until they exceed what it can handle. That overload puts a heavier burden on the liver. The toxins move into the bloodstream and set off systemic inflammation throughout the body, which in turn gives rise to allergies and autoimmune problems.

The second cause is that our body has to help us clear those toxins, and at the same time it has to repair our damaged cells every single day.

So when toxins are in excess and nutrition falls short, a strange situation appears. You look like someone who eats a full meal every day, yet your cells are starving. Behind this hidden hunger are two things happening at once: your intake of the bulk nutrients is excessive, while your intake of the micronutrients is seriously deficient. The nutrition is out of balance. The World Health Organization gave this a name, [[hidden hunger|隐性饥饿, the WHO and FAO term for micronutrient deficiency: a person may eat enough calories, or too many, while still lacking the vitamins and minerals the body needs in small amounts.|https://www.who.int/health-topics/micronutrients]]. What causes it? The slide lists many factors. Genetics accounts for 40 percent. Many people say, "It runs in my family." But none of us had it at the start, and our grandparents did not have the three highs. What gets passed down is not only genes. Far more of it is lifestyle, diet, and daily habits, including smoking and drinking. Then there is the state of mind in a society where everyone is under constant competitive pressure. There is the environment we live in. I am in the north of China, and the natural environment where I live is not good. If you grew up in a place of war, your environment is worse still. And there is the food we eat and what is in it. We all know about pesticides, chemical fertilizers, and all the antibiotics and growth promoters in use, all of which leave us facing toxin overload.

On top of that, there is a very large group of people who, once diagnosed, take medication for life. There is an old Chinese saying that every medicine carries some poison. Having said all this, think about it: is it really possible for you to avoid a toxin overload in your body? I believe every one of us faces this problem. The final result of toxin overload plus the hidden hunger of every cell is that the function of the cell breaks down. When cell function breaks down, the function of the organ made of those cells breaks down. Only then do the symptoms of disease appear. That is the entire root logic of disease.
