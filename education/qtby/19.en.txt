The same is true of high blood pressure. Many people, the moment they are diagnosed, go to the hospital and start blood pressure medication, and that becomes a lifetime of pills, with many outcomes none of us want to see. What does disease nutrition see instead?

Before it is joined by atherosclerosis, the condition is simply elevated blood pressure. It is not yet hypertension. Most people are actually at this stage. We know that true blood pressure elevation happens because fat metabolism in the body has gone wrong: cholesterol is over the limit, oxidized lipids are deposited on the inner walls of the vessels, inflammation sets in, and blood pressure rises temporarily as a result.

At this stage, if we solve the cholesterol problem, which is the fat metabolism problem, elevated blood pressure returns to normal quite easily. And whose problem is that? Where is fat metabolized? Again, the liver. In other words, when the liver is well, elevated blood pressure is fairly easy to reverse naturally and effectively. But once blood pressure medication has been started and atherosclerosis has set in, it is a different matter. It becomes true [[hypertension|The World Health Organization defines hypertension as blood pressure of 140 over 90 or higher measured on two separate days. Elevated pressure in the cerebral vessels combined with carotid plaque can lead to stroke, whether a blockage or a bleed.

We are all familiar with the stroke that ends in paralysis on one side. The rate of disability and the loss of quality of life are enormous. And when atherosclerosis of the coronary arteries is combined with high blood pressure, we get what we usually call coronary heart disease, which can progress to heart attack and sudden death, with a very high fatality rate.
