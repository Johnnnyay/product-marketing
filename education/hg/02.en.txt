Since we have come to urination, let me now talk about the two eliminations, the large and small, the stool and urine. Many friends come to me saying, I am constipated, what do I do? They want to cleanse the intestines and purify the colon. You will find that if you only regulate and purify the intestines, the moment you stop the cleansing plan, the constipation comes back. Why?

Because you have not found the root cause. Chinese medicine says the kidney governs the two eliminations, meaning the kidney is the commander in charge of stool and urine.
