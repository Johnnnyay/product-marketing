Root Cause Analysis
Hydration
500–1000 ml per day is roughly one-quarter of what Jocelyne's body needs at her current weight. Chronic under-hydration at this level directly affects hair follicle cycling, skin barrier function, headache frequency, digestion, and cognitive clarity. This is the single change that costs nothing and affects the broadest range of her symptoms.
Inflammation & Recovery
Hair loss, facial acne, severe period pain, heavy flow, and excess weight together signal elevated systemic inflammation. Excess body weight produces pro-inflammatory molecules that disrupt the hormonal pathways governing the menstrual cycle, skin sebum production, and hair follicle growth. With near-zero produce and no confirmed omega-3 source, the body's resolution pathway — its ability to turn inflammation off — is operating with very little input. This is a nutrient supply problem with a direct fix.
Blood Sugar & Fiber Gap
At 0–1 servings of produce per day, dietary fiber is near zero. Without fiber, blood glucose rises and falls without a brake — producing the occasional sweet and salty cravings and binge eating she reported. Fiber is also one of the most reliable tools for hunger management and satiety, and its absence means she's managing appetite without its most practical ally.
Nutrient Gaps
A multivitamin is a good start (consistency to confirm in session). The gaps a standard multivitamin doesn't fill: omega-3, dietary fiber, and the phytonutrients and antioxidants that come only from actual produce. At 0–1 servings per day, the plant compounds that support hormonal clearance, skin repair, and hair cycling are nearly absent.
Summary
Jocelyne's profile is one where the foundation — water, produce, and omega-3 — is almost entirely absent, and the symptoms she experiences are the predictable result. The severe menstrual pain, hair loss, acne, and emotional regulation difficulty are not separate problems; they are different expressions of the same underlying inflammation and hormonal imbalance. The encouraging part: she sleeps early, maintains good protein habits, and has healthy gut motility. Those are real strengths to build from, and these patterns respond well when given the right inputs consistently.
30-Day Plan
1Water First
  • Target 2L daily to start, building toward 3L by week 4
  • One full glass immediately upon waking, before anything else
  • Keep a water bottle visible at all times — refill at every meal
  • Three sips every time you sit down at a screen
2Produce on Every Plate
  • Add one serving of fruit or vegetables to every meal — minimum 3 servings immediately
  • Morning smoothie: efficient way to get 2 servings before 9 AM
  • Target 4–5 servings by week 4
3Menstrual & Hormonal Support
  • Take omega-3 daily with dinner
  • Reduce refined sugar in the week before your period — directly lowers prostaglandin production and cramping
  • Track pain rating and flow heaviness at next cycle to measure progress
4Movement
  • Stand or walk 5 minutes every hour during screen time
  • One 15–20 minute walk daily — consistency over intensity
Support Tools (Optional)

With near-zero produce and no fish intake, this gap is reliable. Also supports the hair and skin inflammation pattern.

Prebiotic Fiber
At 0–1 servings of produce per day, dietary fiber is critically low. Prebiotic fiber stabilizes blood sugar, reduces appetite spikes, supports gut bacteria that regulate inflammation and hormonal clearance, and directly improves satiety — one of the most reliable tools for managing hunger without aggressive calorie cutting.
Take with a full glass of water. Fiber needs hydration to work — pair with the water habit above.
