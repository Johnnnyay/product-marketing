Hosting & Participant Guide

How we run the night: the timing, what each role does, how to introduce yourself, and how to host a room that stays sharp and on time.

Built from the JL Team FNH Hosting Guidelines (June 2026) and set to our own Friday night.

Timing

Part of the nightPlan for
Introduction & Recognition5 minutes
Calendar30 seconds per event
Every topic15 seconds for the transition and the speaker's intro, on top of the topic's own time
Personal growth share2 minutes per person
Panel (example: 4 panelists, 15 minutes)1 minute intro, about 3:20 per panelist, 30 second wrap-up
Monthly recognition share60 seconds per person

Arrive by 7:10 PM ET for networking. We start at 7:30 sharp, and everyone is out of the room by 9:30.

For everyone

  • Be on time. Networking starts at 7:10 PM ET. Use the restroom, grab water and wrap up conversations by 7:25 so we start at 7:30 with everyone seated.
  • Dress the part. Sharp business casual. See the dress code guide.
  • On Zoom: camera on, a professional background, and real engagement in the chat (reactions, emojis, wins).
  • When you speak, lead with your main point first, then one or two sentences of context. Stay inside your time.

Introducing yourself (candidates and new IBOs)

In this orderName · City · Background · One line on what excites you about the opportunity

Weekly recognition (existing partners)

In this order, and only these pointsName · Background · Sponsor (if you know it) · Coach / Mentor · What you did this week (2 MG1 or 20+ PV retail) · Goal

New apprentice orientation

In this orderName · City · Who you are building with · What you are excited about · One fun fact about yourself

Personal growth share (2 minutes)

Book or audioWhat you read or listened to · 1 or 2 major takeaways · 1 or 2 things you are implementing

Roles

Host & Co-host

Run the room

  • Greet people at the door from 7:10 so partners and guests feel welcome.
  • 7:25: call the personal needs break (restroom, water, seats).
  • Set the tone in the first 60 seconds: smile, project, give the room one focus.
  • Stay front and center and never let the room go quiet between parts.
  • Introduce every speaker with Who and So What.
  • Watch the clock. If timing slips, check with your coach right away on where to cut.
  • Split the segments with your co-host before the night, and hand off with a clear cue.
Online CEO

Chief Energy Officer

  • At 7:30, post the expectations in the Zoom chat: camera on, professional background, chat etiquette.
  • Tell the online audience how to join in (reactions, emojis) and keep the chat moving: links, wins, shout-outs.
  • Camera off? Send a direct message. If nothing changes after a few minutes, remove them to protect the standard.
  • Watch the participant list, mute background noise, rename accounts for clarity.
  • Make sure online guests hear the room clearly and feel part of it.
Tech

Sound, screen, timers

  • Music before the start and during transitions to keep the room lively; fade it at 7:30.
  • Slides, Zoom stream and audio set up before 7:10.
  • Keep a countdown visible on Zoom for online speakers, and time cards (2 minutes, 1 minute, wrap up) for the room.
  • Record talks from leaders.

Hosting skills

Who and So What: introducing a speaker

Every introduction answers two things, in about 15 seconds:

  1. Who: their name, and something about them that connects them to the room.
  2. So What: why the room needs to hear them on this topic, right now.

"Next up is Sarah. The reason you want to listen to her tonight is that she streamlined her whole weekly process while working 50+ hours a week, and she is going to break down that exact system for us right now."

The takeaway bridge: smoother transitions

Skip empty lines like "Thanks for sharing, next up we have…". Take one word or point from the last speaker, affirm it, and use it to open the next part.

"I love what Mike said about consistency being the compound interest of our business. Speaking of consistency, our next section is about the daily habits it takes to scale…"

Keeping time

  • Politely cut off anyone who runs well over, except the final wrap-up speaker.
  • Spend recognition time on what is new: new qualifiers and promotions, not events we covered in past weeks.
  • At the close, thank the people who made the night run: Tech, Online CEO, everyone at the desk.

The calendar

30 seconds per event. It is not a logistics readout: sell the value, the vision and the room.

  • Show the room: a picture from the last one (the crowd, the stage, the networking). People connect with places, not bullet points.
  • Your own story: one line on why you never miss it, or what shifted for you the first time.
  • Why it matters: what someone gains by being there.
  • Who is in the room: the leaders and mentors partners don't usually get access to.

"Look at this picture from the last conference. The reason this next one matters is that it's your chance to hear from our top leaders how they built theirs. My first time in that room changed how I looked at my weekly tracker. Here is how to qualify…"

Panels

The huddle

Meet the panelists 10 to 15 minutes before the start. Confirm the order of questions, and ask everyone to give their main point first, then one or two sentences of support.

Timing math (4 panelists, 15 minutes)

WhenWhat happens
0:00 to 1:00The host sets up the topic and why it matters to the room now.
1:00 to 14:30Each panelist, about 3:20: the host introduces them (Who and So What) and asks; they confirm name and background in 10 seconds, then answer, bottom line first.
14:30 to 15:00The host closes with one strong takeaway and moves straight to the next part.

Between panelists, don't reset: take one point from the last answer and hand it straight to the next person.

"Sarah, that idea of tracking your numbers daily is huge. John, building on that same discipline, how did you stay on track when you were fighting for your first qualification?"

Run of show

Before the night (hosts)

  • Go through the agenda in full.
  • Write a Who and So What intro for every speaker and panelist.
  • Build the slides: weekly recognition, monthly recognition on the first Friday of the month, and the calendar.
  • Agree with your co-host on who introduces which part.

On the night

  • 7:00 PMHosts and Tech arrive, 30 minutes before the start: slides, sound, Zoom, timers.
  • 7:10 PMDoors open and networking starts. Hosts greet at the door.
  • 7:25 PMPersonal needs call: restroom, water, take your seats.
  • 7:30 PMMusic fades, Zoom unmuted, start on time. Kick off with why we gather every week, then Introduction & Recognition (5 minutes). Call on anyone who missed it so they can introduce themselves.
  • AfterEach topic with a 15 second transition and intro. The calendar at 30 seconds per event.
  • CloseThank the teams, and remind everyone we leave the room by 9:30 PM.

Monthly recognition

First FNH of each month only. Leaders send in names and photos by Thursday night so the slides can be set. A partner with several pins is recognized once, on one slide. Coaches prepare their people beforehand so every share fits in 60 seconds.

Stand and wave (no microphone)

Say what the achievement means, call the names, and have them stand for applause:

  • New Partner
  • SSI Bonus 3/12 and 6/12
  • New 100 to 300 PV
  • New Phase B
  • 20 Process Meeting Club, 8 MG Club, Double, Triple and Quadruple Core

One-minute share (on stage or unmuted)

  • SSI Bonus 12/12: name, coach or mentor, and their goal or takeaway.
  • New Core Run finishers: name, coach or mentor, and their number one tip.
  • New 600 to 6000 PV, 3/6, 6/15, BFI and BBI: name, coach or mentor, and the mindset shift that got them there.

Life wins

Celebrate them from the stage, without handing over the mic. Frame them as what makes this community different: the discipline, accountability and people skills built here carry into health, relationships and careers.

主持与参与指南

一个周五晚上怎样办好：时间怎么把控，每个角色各管什么，自我介绍怎么讲，主持人又如何让全场始终在状态、准时推进。

根据 JL 团队 FNH 主持指南（2026 年 6 月）整理，并按我们自己的周五晚上做了调整。

时间安排

环节预留时间
介绍与表彰5 分钟
活动日历每个活动 30 秒
每个议题转场加介绍讲者 15 秒，算在议题本身时间之外
个人成长分享每人 2 分钟
圆桌分享（以 4 位嘉宾、15 分钟为例）开场 1 分钟，每位嘉宾约 3 分 20 秒，收尾 30 秒
月度表彰分享每人 60 秒

美东时间晚上 7:10 前到场交流。7:30 准时开始，9:30 前全员离场。

全体须知

  • 准时到场。美东时间 7:10 开始交流。洗手间、倒水、聊天都在 7:25 前收好，7:30 全员落座，准时开场。
  • 着装得体。干净利落的商务休闲装。查看着装指南。
  • 线上参加：打开摄像头，用专业背景，在聊天区真正参与进来（表情回应、晒成果）。
  • 轮到你发言，先说结论，再用一两句话交代背景，不超出自己的时间。

自我介绍（候选人和新 IBO）

按这个顺序姓名 · 城市 · 背景 · 一句话说说这个机会最让你兴奋的地方

每周表彰（现有伙伴）

按这个顺序，只讲这几点姓名 · 背景 · 推荐人（知道的话）· 教练 / 导师 · 本周做了什么（2 个 MG1 或 20+ PV 零售）· 目标

新学徒迎新会

按这个顺序姓名 · 城市 · 和谁一起做 · 最期待的是什么 · 一件关于自己的趣事

个人成长分享（2 分钟）

一本书或一段音频读了或听了什么 · 一到两个最大的收获 · 正在落实的一到两件事

角色分工

主持 & 联合主持

掌控全场

  • 7:10 起在门口迎接，让伙伴和来宾一进门就感到被欢迎。
  • 7:25 提醒大家方便一下：洗手间、倒水、入座。
  • 开场 60 秒定下基调：面带笑容，声音放出去，给全场一个焦点。
  • 始终站在台前，环节之间不冷场。
  • 介绍每一位讲者，都用 Who 和 So What。
  • 盯紧时间。一旦超时，马上和教练商量从哪里压缩。
  • 会前和联合主持分好环节，交接时给出清楚的提示。
线上 CEO

首席能量官

  • 7:30 在 Zoom 聊天区发出参会要求：开摄像头、专业背景、聊天礼仪。
  • 告诉线上的朋友怎么参与（表情回应等），让聊天区一直热闹：发链接、晒成果、点名鼓励。
  • 有人没开摄像头？先私信提醒。几分钟后还没改变，就请出会议，守住标准。
  • 留意参会名单，静音背景噪音，把账号改成一看就懂的名字。
  • 确保线上来宾听得清现场，也有参与感。
技术

音响、屏幕、计时

  • 开场前和转场时放音乐，让现场保持热度，7:30 音乐渐弱。
  • 7:10 前调好幻灯片、Zoom 直播和音响。
  • Zoom 上给线上讲者显示倒计时，现场用计时卡提醒（2 分钟、1 分钟、请收尾）。
  • 领导人的分享要录下来。

主持技巧

Who 和 So What：怎么介绍讲者

每一次介绍都要在 15 秒左右讲清两件事：

  1. Who：他是谁，以及他和在座各位的连接点。
  2. So What：为什么此时此刻、在这个话题上，大家要听他讲。

“接下来有请 Sarah。今晚大家一定要认真听她分享：她每周工作 50 多个小时，却把自己一整周的流程理得井井有条。现在，她就把这套方法原原本本拆给我们看。”

借力转场：让衔接更顺

别再说“谢谢分享，下面有请……”这类空话。从上一位讲者的话里拎出一个词或一个观点，先肯定它，再顺势引出下一个环节。

“Mike 说得太好了，坚持就是我们这份事业的复利。说到坚持，下一个环节讲的，正是把事业做大所需要的日常习惯……”

把控时间

  • 有人明显超时，礼貌地打断，压轴总结的讲者除外。
  • 表彰时间留给新的成就：新达标、新晋升。前几周讲过的活动不再重复。
  • 结尾感谢让这个晚上顺利进行的人：技术、线上 CEO，以及签到台的每一位。

活动日历

每个活动 30 秒。这不是念行程，而是讲价值、讲愿景、讲现场。

  • 让人看见现场：放一张上一次的照片（人潮、舞台、交流的画面）。打动人的是场景，不是要点。
  • 你自己的故事：一句话讲你为什么从不缺席，或者第一次去时你有什么改变。
  • 为什么值得去：到场的人能带走什么。
  • 谁会在场：那些平时伙伴们很难接触到的领导人和导师。

“大家看这张上次大会的照片。下一场之所以重要，是因为你能当面听顶尖领导人讲他们是怎么做起来的。我第一次坐进那个会场，回来之后看每周追踪表的眼光就完全不一样了。达标方式是这样的……”

圆桌分享

会前碰头

开始前 10 到 15 分钟和嘉宾碰一下。确认提问顺序，请每位嘉宾先讲核心观点，再用一两句话支撑。

时间怎么分（4 位嘉宾，15 分钟）

时间内容
0:00 到 1:00主持人引出话题，讲清为什么现在讲这个对大家重要。
1:00 到 14:30每位嘉宾约 3 分 20 秒：主持人用 Who 和 So What 介绍并提问；嘉宾 10 秒内报上姓名和背景，然后作答，结论先行。
14:30 到 15:00主持人用一句有力的收获收尾，直接进入下一个环节。

嘉宾之间不要重新起头：从上一位的回答里拎出一个点，直接交给下一位。

“Sarah，每天追踪数据这一点太关键了。John，同样是这份自律，你当初冲第一个达标的时候，是怎么稳住节奏的？”

当晚流程

会前准备（主持人）

  • 把整份议程完整过一遍。
  • 给每位讲者和嘉宾写好 Who 和 So What 介绍词。
  • 做好幻灯片：每周表彰、月度表彰（每月第一个周五）和活动日历。
  • 和联合主持说好谁介绍哪个环节。

当晚

  • 7:00 PM主持和技术提前 30 分钟到场：幻灯片、音响、Zoom、计时器。
  • 7:10 PM开门，交流开始。主持人在门口迎接。
  • 7:25 PM提醒大家：洗手间、倒水、入座。
  • 7:30 PM音乐渐弱，Zoom 取消静音，准时开场。先讲我们为什么每周相聚，再进入介绍与表彰（5 分钟）。没赶上介绍的人，请他补上。
  • 之后每个议题留 15 秒转场和介绍。活动日历每个活动 30 秒。
  • 收尾感谢各个团队，提醒大家 9:30 前离场。

月度表彰

只在每月第一次 FNH 进行。领导人在周四晚上前把名单和照片发过来，方便做幻灯片。同一位伙伴拿了好几个奖章，只表彰一次，放在同一张幻灯片上。教练提前帮自己的伙伴准备好，每人分享控制在 60 秒内。

起立挥手（不上麦）

讲清这项成就意味着什么，念出名字，请他们起立接受掌声：

  • New Partner 新伙伴
  • SSI 奖金 3/12 和 6/12
  • 新达 100 到 300 PV
  • 新晋 Phase B
  • 20 次 Process Meeting 俱乐部、8 次 MG 俱乐部、双倍 / 三倍 / 四倍 Core

一分钟分享（上台或线上开麦）

  • SSI 奖金 12/12：姓名、教练或导师，以及目标或收获。
  • 新完成 Core Run：姓名、教练或导师，以及最想分享的一条心得。
  • 新达 600 到 6000 PV、3/6、6/15、BFI 和 BBI：姓名、教练或导师，以及让他们走到这一步的心态转变。

生活中的喜讯

在台上替他们庆祝，但不把麦克风交出去。借这个机会讲出这个团队的不同：在这里练出来的自律、担当和与人相处的能力，会一路带进健康、感情和事业里。
